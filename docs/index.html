CountMe

Attendance tracker app for Android

CountMe is a free Android app for students and professionals who need to keep track of class or work attendance. You add your subjects, mark each class as present or absent, and CountMe shows your attendance percentage and tells you how many classes you can still miss, or must attend, to stay above your required percentage.

What CountMe does

  • Track attendance per subject, with a target percentage you choose.
  • See at a glance which subjects are on track and which are below target.
  • Get a clear answer to "how many classes can I safely miss?"
  • Keep a dated history of every class you marked.
  • Export your data as JSON, CSV or PDF at any time.

Your data stays yours

CountMe has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your attendance data is stored on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, CountMe also keeps a readable copy in a folder named CountMe in your own Google Drive, so your records survive a lost phone and sync between your devices. The developer cannot see your data.

How CountMe uses your Google account

Signing in is optional. When you do, CountMe asks for one permission, drive.file, which lets the app see and edit only the files it created itself in your Drive. It cannot read your other files. CountMe also reads your Google account name and email address once, only to show you which account is connected. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.
